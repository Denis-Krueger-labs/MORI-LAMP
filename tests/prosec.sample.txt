TITLE: Junior Pentester / Red Teamer (m/w/d)
COMPANY: ProSec GmbH
LOCATION: Mendig, Hybrid
EMPLOYMENT_TYPE: Vollzeit, Festanstellung

ROLE

Junior employees learn the complete project lifecycle, including scoping,
execution, documentation, and presenting results.

- Take ownership of limited test areas under senior guidance.
- Participate in technical, social, and physical security engagements.
- Develop focus areas such as web, mobile, cloud, Active Directory,
  initial access, social engineering, or physical access.
- Prepare findings and present them with the project lead.
- Share technical knowledge through articles or videos.

PROFILE

- Support the company's mission and security approach.
- Have several years of IT administration experience or equivalent
  independently acquired knowledge of networks and infrastructure.
- Have practical penetration-testing or red-team experience. Accepted
  examples include CTFs, Hack The Box, a homelab, OSCP preparation,
  or documented personal projects.
- Demonstrate initiative, proactive thinking, and responsibility.
- Communicate technical risks clearly to customers and collaborate well.
- Speak German at minimum C1 and English at minimum B2.
- Some classified engagements may require eligibility for a security review.
- Offensive-security certifications such as OSCP, CRTP, CRTO I/II,
  HTB CPTS, or comparable qualifications are desirable.

APPLICATION RULES

- AI may be used as a tool, but the application must contain genuine
  personal input.
- Apply only to positions that genuinely match the candidate.
